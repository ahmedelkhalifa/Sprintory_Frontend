About Scrum, Kanban and workspaces in Sprintory.

  • Google sign-in

Can one workspace run Scrum and Kanban?

Yes. The template is chosen per project, so Scrum projects with sprints and Kanban projects with WIP limits sit side by side.

Who can complete or cancel a Scrum sprint?

Completing takes the right to manage sprints. Cancelling needs a written reason and is left to the Product Owner or Project Manager.

How do I bring my team into Sprintory?

Invite people by email. They open the link, sign in with that address or with Google, and land in the workspace.

How do GitHub and Google Calendar join a project?

Each connects to a project, so its code and its dates sit next to its tasks.

What does a workspace admin see across projects?

Admins manage members and their roles, see every project and read an audit log of key actions.
